Hi, I'm Akash.

I'm a Business Systems Analyst with experience improving lending platforms and operational systems in financial services.

At Cash4You, I worked across the full lending lifecycle — from loan origination and underwriting to funding and collections. My work focused on analyzing system behavior, improving workflows, and helping teams deliver reliable solutions that support daily branch operations.

I collaborate closely with operations teams, risk teams, and developers to translate business problems into clear requirements, process maps, and testable system improvements. Much of my work involves investigating system issues, analyzing data, validating API behavior, and ensuring new changes work smoothly for the teams using these systems every day.

Open to relocation. Available for remote, hybrid, and on-site roles.
